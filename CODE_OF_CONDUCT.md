# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want DIAMANDIS ONE to be a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Give feedback focused on ideas and code, not personal attributes.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may block participants who violate this policy. Report concerns privately through GitHub's repository contact or security reporting options.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community channels.
